# ARCHMAGE Contributor Ranks

ARCHMAGE ranks make contribution trust visible without requiring membership in
saengil.ai. A rank is granted by a maintainer after identity, alignment, scope,
and evidence-readiness review.

Ranks are not employment, organizational membership, ownership, or a permanent
right to contribute. Maintainers may change or revoke a rank when evidence,
conduct, security, or project alignment requires it.

## Repository permissions

B-Rank, A-Rank, and S-Rank Archmages contribute from forks and do not receive
repository write or merge access solely because of rank. SS-Rank Archmage is the
only rank eligible for maintainer access. The initial repository collaborator
and final merger is `@YXZYSME`; any later maintainer is selected explicitly by
the existing maintainer.

## B-Rank Archmage

An approved contributor for a bounded issue.

- Works only on the named proposal and approved scope.
- Suitable for focused bug fixes, tests, documentation, or contained adapters.
- Approval expires after 30 days unless renewed.

GitHub label: `B-Rank Archmage`

## A-Rank Archmage

A trusted contributor with a record of accepted, evidence-backed work.

- May take on broader adapters, language integrations, or multi-file changes.
- Demonstrates reliable testing, documentation, and review responsiveness.
- Still requires issue-bound approval for each contribution.

GitHub label: `A-Rank Archmage`

## S-Rank Archmage

A senior contributor trusted with high-impact systems work.

- May be approved for core policy, security, performance, or research changes.
- Demonstrates strong architectural judgment and reproducible evidence.
- Does not receive merge or maintainer authority automatically.

GitHub label: `S-Rank Archmage`

## SS-Rank Archmage

An ARCHMAGE maintainer with final repository authority.

- May approve proposals, assign or revoke ranks, and merge pull requests.
- Owns release, security, compatibility, and design-integrity decisions.
- Must preserve the same CI and evidence gates applied to community work.

GitHub label: `SS-Rank Archmage`
