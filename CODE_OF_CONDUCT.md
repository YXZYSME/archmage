# Community Conduct

ARCHMAGE contributors are expected to make technical collaboration safe,
constructive, evidence-led, and useful to the agentic ecosystem.

- Discuss the work, not the worth of the person doing it.
- Give review feedback that is specific, actionable, and proportional.
- Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, or expose private information.
- Do not use project channels for spam, discrimination, or sexualized conduct.
- Respect coordinated vulnerability disclosure and embargoed security details.
- Disclose agentic-tool use and provide sanitized prompt evidence when required.
- Do not fabricate tests, benchmarks, identities, approvals, provenance, or
  compatibility claims.
- Keep proposals within their approved scope and accept maintainer decisions
  without pressure, brigading, or repeated unsolicited resubmission.
- Do not use ARCHMAGE channels primarily for promotion, recruiting, or unrelated
  advocacy.

## Maintainer authority

Maintainers may label, edit, lock, close, or remove contributions and restrict
participation when behavior threatens contributors, users, the project, or its
design integrity. Maintainers are not required to publicly explain a rejection,
rank decision, moderation action, or non-selection. Special cases may receive a
private or public explanation at maintainer discretion.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, security
reports, project events, and other spaces where a participant represents
ARCHMAGE.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to `yxzys@proton.me`. Include links, dates, and
the minimum supporting information needed to evaluate the report. Reports will
be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or
witness is prohibited.
